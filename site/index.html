How it started

It began with a spreadsheet and a ten-year-old.

As a schoolboy in Estonia, I read about laying the history of the Universe on the road from Tallinn to Tartu, a road every Estonian knows by seat. Decades later I put the timeline in an Excel file, put my son in the car, and scrolled as we drove.

It was the best trip to Tartu either of us ever had.

Ott, founder
