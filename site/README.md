# Website prototype

Live: https://ottsme.github.io/time_machine/site/

A working mockup of the new timetravelco.com home page, for tuning the look and the headline before the real site is built.

**The headline** rolls the story lengths in one clean sentence: *Make any road / 13.8 billion · 4.5 billion · 300,000 / years long.* After the three numbers, "road" swaps to "walk" on the big beat (the moment 13.8 billion rolls in), and the cycle runs again. Everything moves down: the new item comes in from above. Each 13.8 billion holds a little longer, so the three-beat cycle is readable.

- On a large screen the tuning panel sits on the right. On a phone, tap the round button bottom right to open it.
- Tuned values are kept on the device. "Copy values" puts the current numbers on the clipboard for implementation, including the length of one full loop.
- Installable: open it on the phone and choose "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app"). When online it always loads the newest version.
- "Restart roll" starts the headline from the top, to judge the timing.
- People who turn motion off in their system settings (e.g. Windows "Animation effects") get a crossfade instead of the slide: same rhythm, nothing moves. "Change style" in the panel forces slide or fade, to compare them on any device. Screen readers get the whole sentence with all three numbers.

**Stand-ins:** the story pictures are old reference frames from the menu prototype; the rings in the hero stand in for the app's real spacetime tunnel; other pictures are labelled placeholders until deck images are chosen. The founder story is a draft for Ott to rewrite.

Parked ideas (not built): "Every road is 13.8 billion years long" (one story only), "Turn any road into a time machine", a rotating hero with a picture per story, cartoon placards (rejected).
