# Story menu prototype

Live: https://ottsme.github.io/time_machine/menu/

A working mockup of the new story menu: one card per story, opening like an accordion. The idea is to show the road itself before the trip. A closed card shows its story's eras as a thin colour strip along the bottom edge. When the card opens, it shows a short overview of what the story is about, the number of events and the Start button.

**No spoilers, no quizzes (v1.2).** The overview says what the story covers, never where things fall on the road. The road to scale, the era legend and the milestones are still in the Layout panel, off by default.

- On a phone the screen fills the whole display. Tap the gear to open the tune sheet.
- On a large screen it shows a 390 × 844 phone frame with the tune panels beside it, the same as the HUD prototype.
- EN / ET switches the whole menu. In English, the Republic of Estonia card says "In Estonian".
- Tuned values are kept on the device. "Copy values" puts the current numbers on the clipboard for implementation.
- **The pictures are stand-ins** (old reference frames from the repo root, plus two HUD frames) until each deck's heading picture is chosen. Estonia has no picture yet and always uses a drawn stand-in.
- Era bands and milestones are road shares (0–100 %), taken from the decks. The overview texts are drafts.
